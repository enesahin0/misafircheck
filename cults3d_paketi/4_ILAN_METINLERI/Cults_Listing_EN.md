## Title
Turkey 81 Provinces Puzzle Map — Removable Provinces, Engraved Names, Fixed Seas & Neighbouring Countries (3D Print Set)

## Short description
Print all 81 provinces of Türkiye as individual puzzle pieces that sit in one continuous map-shaped pocket. Province names are engraved; the seas, islands and neighbouring countries are part of the fixed base.

## Description
**Turkey 81 Provinces Puzzle** is a 3D-printable map for education, gifts and decoration. Each of the 81 provinces is a separate piece that drops into a **single continuous pocket** shaped like Türkiye.

### What is included
- **81 provinces, 81 separate STL files** (01_Adana … 81_Duzce).
- **Base** — the Türkiye pocket plus the seas (Black Sea, Aegean, Mediterranean, Marmara), islands and neighbouring countries (Greece, Bulgaria, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan/Nakhchivan, Iran, Iraq, Syria, Cyprus) as **one fixed body**. Thin border grooves separate the neighbouring countries.
- **4-part base** for small print beds (each part 210 × 106 × 6 mm), glued together.
- **Engraved province names**, 0.4 mm deep, with full Turkish characters (Ç, Ğ, İ, I, Ö, Ş, Ü).
- **Rounded top edges** — 0.4 mm, 4-segment bevel.
- **One pocket** — no province borders carved into the base.
- **Assembly clearance** — the pocket is 0.25 mm larger per side than the assembled map; neighbouring provinces share their borders exactly.
- All units are **millimetres**, STL files are 1:1 scale; all meshes are closed (watertight) and manifold.

### Dimensions
| Part | Size |
|---|---|
| Base (single piece) | 420 × 212 × 6 mm |
| Base (4 parts) | 4 × 210 × 106 × 6 mm |
| Province pieces | 5 mm tall (3 mm sits in the pocket, 2 mm stands proud) |
| Total solid volume | ≈ 578 cm³ |

The full base needs a print bed of at least **430 × 220 mm**. Use the 4-part base for smaller beds (at least 215 × 110 mm).

### Suggested print settings (recommendations)
- Print pieces **flat side down** (z=0 is flat) — no supports needed.
- Layer height 0.12–0.20 mm; 0.4 mm nozzle (0.25–0.3 mm gives crisper text on small provinces).
- 3 perimeters, 10–20% infill.
- If pieces fit too tightly: enable elephant-foot compensation (0.1–0.2 mm) or lightly sand the bottom edge.
- Fill the engraved letters with acrylic paint and wipe the surface for high-contrast names.

### Notes
- **Yalova** is too small to carry any text.
- For **Zonguldak and Osmaniye** the plate code (67, 80) is engraved instead of the name; the other 78 provinces carry their names.
- Educational/decorative map, not an official map. Borders are simplified for printability (source resolution ≈ 1–2 km). Only islands large enough to print (17) are included. The Bosphorus and Dardanelles are not shown as separate channels at this scale.
